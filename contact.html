Hiring

ION graduate students · 2026 rotation

We are looking for one graduate student from the 2026 rotation cohort to join the lab!

One research direction is to record neural activity across multiple brain regions while macaques perform a broad battery of cognitive tasks. We aim to identify neural mechanisms that are shared across tasks. A second direction is to use brain–computer interfaces (BCIs) to modulate cognitive functions in macaques. The work will combine experiments and computational modeling.

Undergraduate students

Lab life and mentoring

Our lab currently has one postdoc, six graduate students, one research assistant, and one lab manager (as of 2026 fall).

  • Weekly one-on-one meetings with the PI
  • A weekly lab meeting for journal club, non-progress reports, etc.
  • Monthly lab dinners and an annual lab outing

We value careful experimental design, reproducible analysis, and collaboration. The PI provides hands-on guidance early on and supports members as they develop independent research and plan their next career steps.
